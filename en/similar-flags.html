👯 Similar Flags – The Biggest Confusions

Do you know the subtle differences between almost identical country flags?

Why do some flags look so similar?

There are dozens of country flags that look confusingly similar. The reasons are diverse: shared history, similar political movements, geographic proximity, or simply coincidence. These similar flags challenge even geography experts in the flag quiz.

In this comprehensive guide, we show you the most confusing flag pairs and groups in the world and explain how you can reliably tell them apart. Perfect for improving your flag knowledge and never guessing the wrong flag again!

🔴 The 10 most confused flag pairs

1. 🇮🇩 Indonesia vs 🇲🇨 Monaco

Similarity: 99% – Almost identical!

🇮🇩 Indonesia

  • Red top, White bottom
  • Aspect ratio: 2:3
  • Slightly wider format
  • Since 1945 (Independence)

🇲🇨 Monaco

  • Red top, White bottom
  • Aspect ratio: 4:5
  • More square
  • Official since 1881

💡 Distinction tip: Monaco is more square, Indonesia more rectangular. In quiz context: Monaco is a tiny city-state in Europe, Indonesia a huge island nation in Asia.

2. 🇷🇴 Romania vs 🇹🇩 Chad

Similarity: 98% – Only minimal color differences

🇷🇴 Romania

  • Blue, Yellow, Red (vertical)
  • Lighter blue (cobalt blue)
  • Since 1989 (after Ceaușescu)
  • European flag

🇹🇩 Chad

  • Blue, Yellow, Red (vertical)
  • Darker blue (indigo blue)
  • Since 1959 (Independence)
  • African flag

💡 Distinction tip: The blue tone is minimally different (Romania lighter). Practically: Romania is in Eastern Europe, Chad in Central Africa.

3. 🇳🇱 Netherlands vs 🇱🇺 Luxembourg

Similarity: 95% – Similar tricolor

🇳🇱 Netherlands

  • Red, White, Blue (horizontal)
  • Darker red
  • Darker blue (cobalt blue)
  • Oldest tricolor (1572)

🇱🇺 Luxembourg

  • Red, White, Light blue (horizontal)
  • Lighter red
  • Clearly lighter blue
  • Official since 1972

💡 Distinction tip: Luxembourg’s blue is much lighter (almost sky blue). Netherlands has a strong dark blue.

4. 🇦🇺 Australia vs 🇳🇿 New Zealand

Similarity: 90% – Both with Union Jack and Southern Cross

🇦🇺 Australia

  • Union Jack + 6 stars
  • Commonwealth Star (7-pointed) under Union Jack
  • 5 white stars (Southern Cross)
  • Darker blue

🇳🇿 New Zealand

  • Union Jack + 4 stars
  • Only Southern Cross
  • 4 red stars with white border
  • Darker blue

💡 Distinction tip: New Zealand has RED stars (4), Australia WHITE stars (6 total: 5 + 1 large).

5. 🇮🇪 Ireland vs 🇨🇮 Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire)

Similarity: 100% mirrored! – Exactly reversed order

🇮🇪 Ireland

  • Green, White, Orange (from left)
  • Green = Catholics
  • Orange = Protestants
  • White = Peace

🇨🇮 Ivory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire)

  • Orange, White, Green (from left)
  • Inspired by French tricolor
  • Reversed order to Ireland
  • West African country

💡 Distinction tip: Ireland: Green LEFT. Ivory Coast: Orange LEFT. Mnemonic: “Ireland is green.”

6. 🇸🇮 Slovenia vs 🇸🇰 Slovakia

Similarity: 85% – Slavic tricolors with coat of arms

🇸🇮 Slovenia

  • White, Blue, Red (horizontal)
  • Coat of arms top left: mountain (Triglav), stars, waves
  • Three yellow stars
  • Part of the Balkans

🇸🇰 Slovakia

  • White, Blue, Red (horizontal)
  • Coat of arms left: double cross on hills
  • White Byzantine cross
  • Part of Central Europe

💡 Distinction tip: Slovenia has a MOUNTAIN and STARS, Slovakia a DOUBLE CROSS.

7. 🇵🇱 Poland vs 🇸🇬 Singapore

Similarity: 80% – Reversed colors

🇵🇱 Poland

  • White top, Red bottom
  • Simple design without symbols
  • Traditional Slavic colors
  • Central European country

🇸🇬 Singapore

  • Red top, White bottom
  • White crescent + 5 stars (left)
  • Asian city-state
  • Southeast Asia

💡 Distinction tip: Poland is simple (no symbols). Singapore has moon and stars.

8. 🇨🇴 Colombia vs 🇪🇨 Ecuador vs 🇻🇪 Venezuela

Similarity: 90% – Gran Colombia heritage

Common

  • All: Yellow, Blue, Red (horizontal)
  • Yellow takes upper half
  • Blue and Red each 1/4
  • Gran Colombia heritage

Differences

  • 🇨🇴 Colombia: No symbols
  • 🇪🇨 Ecuador: Coat of arms center
  • 🇻🇪 Venezuela: 8 stars + coat of arms (optional)

💡 Distinction tip: Colombia = plain, Ecuador = coat of arms, Venezuela = stars.

9. 🇧🇪 Belgium vs 🇩🇪 Germany

Similarity: 75% – Similar colors, different arrangement

🇧🇪 Belgium

  • Black, Yellow, Red (VERTICAL)
  • Almost square (13:15)
  • Benelux state
  • Left to right

🇩🇪 Germany

  • Black, Red, Gold/Yellow (HORIZONTAL)
  • Standard format (3:5)
  • Largest economy in Europe
  • Top to bottom

💡 Distinction tip: Belgium = VERTICAL, Germany = HORIZONTAL. Belgium also has a different color order.

10. 🇳🇴 Norway vs 🇮🇸 Iceland

Similarity: 85% – Nordic cross flags

🇳🇴 Norway

  • Red background
  • Blue cross with white border
  • Scandinavian cross
  • Since 1821

🇮🇸 Iceland

  • Blue background
  • Red cross with white border
  • Scandinavian cross
  • Since 1915

💡 Distinction tip: Norway = red with blue cross. Iceland = blue with red cross. Reversed!

🌍 More similar flag groups

Scandinavian cross flags (5 countries)

All Nordic countries use the asymmetrical Scandinavian cross:

  • 🇩🇰 Denmark: Red with white cross (oldest flag, since 1219)
  • 🇸🇪 Sweden: Blue with yellow cross
  • 🇳🇴 Norway: Red with blue cross (white border)
  • 🇫🇮 Finland: White with blue cross
  • 🇮🇸 Iceland: Blue with red cross (white border)

Pan-Arab colors (12+ countries)

Many Arab countries use red, black, white, and green:

  • 🇯🇴 Jordan, 🇵🇸 Palestine, 🇰🇼 Kuwait, 🇦🇪 UAE
  • 🇸🇩 Sudan, 🇸🇾 Syria, 🇮🇶 Iraq, 🇾🇪 Yemen

Slavic tricolors (6 countries)

White, blue, red in different arrangements:

  • 🇷🇺 Russia: horizontal white-blue-red
  • 🇸🇰 Slovakia: horizontal white-blue-red (with coat of arms)
  • 🇸🇮 Slovenia: horizontal white-blue-red (with coat of arms)
  • 🇨🇿 Czech Republic: white-red with blue triangle
  • 🇷🇸 Serbia: red-blue-white (with coat of arms)

Union Jack flags (4 countries)

Former British colonies with Union Jack:

  • 🇦🇺 Australia, 🇳🇿 New Zealand, 🇫🇯 Fiji, 🇹🇻 Tuvalu

How can I better distinguish similar flags?

  1. Pay attention to symbols: Coats of arms, stars, crosses are often the key
  2. Remember color tones: Light vs dark blue often makes the difference
  3. Learn by regions: Geographic location helps eliminate options
  4. Use mnemonics: “Ireland is green” (green left)
  5. Practice with our quiz: Regular training makes perfect

Frequently asked questions about similar flags

Which are the most similar flags in the world?

Monaco and Indonesia are 99% identical – only the aspect ratio differs. Romania and Chad follow closely behind.

Why do so many countries have similar flags?

Shared history (e.g., Gran Colombia), political movements (Pan-Arabism), geographic proximity (Scandinavia), or coincidence.

Are there legal issues because of similar flags?

Yes! Chad protested Romania’s flag, but both countries kept their designs. There is no international authority for flag designs.

How can I better distinguish similar flags in the quiz?

Focus on details: symbols, color tones, aspect ratio. And use geographic knowledge – context helps eliminate options!
